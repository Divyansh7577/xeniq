Questions

Quick answers.

Does it need internet?

No. Everything runs on your phone, so it works offline in class or in an exam hall (where calculators are allowed).

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. History, notebook lines and settings are saved only on your device. There are no accounts.

Can I use it without installing?

Yes. Open the web version in your browser. On Chrome you can also use Add to Home screen to keep it like an app.

Which tools are included?

School maths (LCM, HCF, fractions, percentages, roots), tables 2 to 50, algebra and calculus, geometry, statistics and probability, science (physics formulas, molar mass, constants, units), everyday and study tools, and computer tools like number bases and truth tables.

Which phones does it work on?

It is built for Android phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape.
